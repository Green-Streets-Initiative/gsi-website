# Polish, enrich and design-lead pass: Home 2 and Donate mockups (2026-11-20)

Mockups only; nothing here is on the live site.

## Home 2, six doors (`home-2-doors-v2.html`)

Screen: home · User: first-time visitor from search or a link, or a returning member · Job: show what GSI offers and get them to one door (an event, a place, a trip, a lesson, an org call, the app) · Next: an event page, a trip, a lesson request, or Donate.

Journeys walked: a newcomer looking for a ride near them; someone planning one trip; an employer or school; a would-be donor; someone who wants the app.

Punch list (ranked) and what changed:
1. Get there showed a made-up trip (Union Square to Fenway Park) as if typed in, so it read as a result, not a form. → Empty fields: "Address or T stop" with "Use where I am", and "Where to?". Fixed.
2. Two different event counts (150 in the hero, 277 on the What's on door) and 64 groups twice. → 150 is the one number; the door link says "See the calendar"; 64 stays only with the logos. Fixed.
3. The hero map had no place names, so a reader couldn't tell whether anything is near them. → Labels for Boston, Lowell, Worcester, Springfield, Pittsfield and Cape Cod. Fixed.
4. "maps of real trips" is our shorthand. → "trail and town maps, and free bike lessons". Fixed.
5. The Get Shift band was the one section with no picture, while it promises live departures. → The real app screenshot (85 bus at Ruggles in 2 min, 8 bikes nearby). Fixed.
6. Four same-shaped rows stacked in the who band (plan, board, money, IRS filings). → One 2×2 grid of tiles. Fixed.
7. Phone: the page ran about 5 screens. → Places and Learn to ride sit side by side on phone, with one credits line under them; now about 4.7 screens. Remaining: the six doors are the page's contents and the menu repeats them, so there are no extra jump links. Keith's call if he wants them.
8. "Spotlight · Community Challenge" label above the challenge name is a kicker. It's the live Spotlight card, shipped and approved, so it's left as is.

## Donate (`donate.html`)

Screen: /donate · User: someone who clicked Donate in the header or the who band · Job: give, monthly by default · Next: Donorbox checkout, then a thank-you that points at this weekend's events.

Journeys walked: monthly giver on a phone; one-time giver; someone checking that GSI is real before giving; someone who would rather mail a check; the moment after the gift.

Punch list (ranked) and what changed:
1. Nothing after the gift: the journey ended at Donorbox. → A thank-you state (`#thanks`): receipt by email, two of this weekend's events, "See what's on". Fixed.
2. The mailing address was missing the suite and box, so a check could go astray. → "519 Somerville Ave, Ste 2, Box 103, Somerville, MA 02143" (the site's own address line), drawn as an envelope. Fixed.
3. "What it pays for" was an icon list (icon plus number together, the AI-template look), and the map was hidden on phone. → Three picture tiles: the events map with 150, the lesson-street photo (credited), and a drawn calendar page with the last Friday marked for Walk/Ride Day. Fixed.
4. "One Friday a month" was wrong. → "Last Friday of every month, since 2006", as the Walk/Ride Days page says. Fixed.
5. Keith's initials sat next to "Green Streets Initiative", and "Questions? Keith Anderson, Executive Director." was a fragment. → "Green Streets Initiative is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 26-1484405. Gifts are tax-deductible." then "Questions? Email Keith Anderson, Executive Director" with his initials. Fixed.
6. The employer-match line promised "many will double your gift". → "Many employers match gifts to nonprofits." Fixed.
