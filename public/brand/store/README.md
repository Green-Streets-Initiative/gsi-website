# Store badges

Official, unmodified badge artwork. Do not recolor, crop, restyle or redraw.

| File | Source | Fetched |
|---|---|---|
| `app-store-badge-black-en-us.svg` | https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/en-us.svg (Apple App Store Marketing Guidelines) | 2026-09-30 |
| `google-play-badge-en.png` | https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png (Google Play Brand Guidelines) | 2026-09-30 |

Usage rules we follow (`src/components/StoreBadges.tsx`):

- Apple first, both badges at the same height, at least 40px on screen.
- Clear space of a quarter of the badge height on every side.
- The black App Store badge is the one to use beside other store badges.
- Credit lines wherever the badges appear: "App Store is a trademark of
  Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries." and "Google Play
  and the Google Play logo are trademarks of Google LLC."
