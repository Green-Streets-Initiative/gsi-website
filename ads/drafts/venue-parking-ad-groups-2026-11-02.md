fenway park parking 1,600, parking near fenway park 1,000. "Not measured"

## Where

- New campaign **Venue Parking**: Massachusetts only (the campaign rule until
  2027), Maximize conversions, $50/day to start, search only. Three ad groups
  meets the two-ad-group minimum. The 5% click-rate floor is account-wide, so
  this campaign has to earn its keep or be paused.
- Sitelinks (campaign level, four): TD Garden parking → /parking/td-garden ·
  Fenway Park parking → /parking/fenway-park · Gillette Stadium parking →
  /parking/gillette-stadium · Plan a trip → /nearby.

## Copy rules for these ads

- Answer the search (parking) in one headline, so the ad is relevant enough to
  be clicked; state the other way in as a plain fact in another ("North
  Station is under the arena"). Never against driving, no "skip the traffic",
  no "ditch the car".
- No prices in the ads. They change by season and event; the page carries
  them with the date checked.
- Ad 1 pins the parking headline to position 1 and the transit fact to
  position 2. Ad 2 is unpinned, so the two can be compared.

## Negative keywords (all three ad groups)

jobs, hiring, careers, tickets, seating chart, seat map, tailgate rentals,
hotel, hotels, address, hours, tours, stadium tour, pro shop, lost and found,
parking ticket, parking tickets, towing, monthly parking, overnight parking

(Monthly and overnight: commuters and residents, not event-goers. "Tickets"
alone: people buying game tickets; it does not block "train tickets" because
negatives are broad, so add "-tickets" only after the first search-terms read
if it cuts the train searches.)

## What counts as a result

- Main, to add (GA4 star, then import as a Primary conversion, same path as
  10-14): `venue_trip_planned`, fired by "Plan your trip to <venue>".
- Already an Ads conversion: the App Store and Google Play badges
  (`shift_store_click`, placement `venue_parking`).
- Tracked, NOT a conversion: `parking_reserve_click` (Ticketmaster, the
  garage's page, SpotHero). Counting it would teach the bidding to find people
  who book parking. It is read in the experiment instead.

## Rule for judging it

Pause any ad group whose click rate is under 5% after 300 impressions. At
these volumes 300 comes in days for Gillette and TD Garden, a week or two for
Fenway (mostly off-season until spring). Read the search terms after the first
week and add negatives.
