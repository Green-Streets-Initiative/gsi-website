## Competitor teardown

**drive-time-conversion — `how far is a 10 minute drive walking`.**
`howlongtowalk.org` wins by answering in its first sentence ("approximately 0.5
miles"), then giving two conversion tables (by pace, and by age), an embedded
calculator, a walk-or-drive breakeven section, a cited source (Bohannon &
Andrews 2011), and internal links to the 15 / 20 / 30-minute variants.
`radiusmapper.com` ranks on the tool alone. **What would beat it:** our new
drive-time guide already leads with the number, which is the important half. It
lacks (a) an actual table — ours is prose-only, (b) sibling pages for the other
durations, so we capture one query where they capture five, and (c) FAQPage
markup. Item 2 fixes the markup on the parent tool page; the table and siblings
are an October candidate.

**students-campus — `MIT T pass student MBTA discount`.**
The MBTA's own page lists 40+ participating schools and gives none of the
details: no prices, no enrollment steps, no per-school specifics — it is a
directory that tells you to ask your school. MIT's own page covers MIT's 50%
subsidy and nothing else. **What would beat this is exactly the shape we already
built:** one page per campus, all the perks in one place — pass subsidy, bike
co-op, Bluebikes discount, shuttle, late-night ride — each with a source. That
is why these pages sit at position 9 with no links and no age. The gap now is
the click, not the rank, which is what exp-2026-09-1 tests.

**year-round-weather — `how do people bike in winter in Boston`.**
Covered in finding 5. Incumbents: Boston.gov, Streetsblog (2022), Boston
Cyclists Union, Landry's. None carry structured data. The winning move is one
comprehensive, genuinely local, current page — not a fourth short guide.

### Item 3 — Micro-guide: what a cargo e-bike costs

**What:** `seo/drafts/guides/what-a-cargo-ebike-costs.md` — new and used price
ranges, what drives the price (motor placement, battery, load rating, brakes), a
five-point checklist for buying secondhand, and where to try one locally.

**Why:** the `used-car-to-ebike` wedge has shown almost nothing since the routine
began, and this is its first repeatable signal: variants of `"tern gsd" used
"$3000"` took 34 impressions at position 9.1 over 28 days, across two
consecutive pulls. `/guides/what-you-can-do-with-an-e-cargo-bike` (102
impressions) answers what a cargo bike does; nothing of ours answers what one
costs.

**Freshness:** bike prices are not in `prices.json` and have no freshness cron,
so the guide uses ranges and price drivers rather than model-by-model figures.
The Massachusetts voucher program — which would have dated fastest — is handled
as a pointer, not a promise: the MassCEC statewide voucher ran April–September
2025 and is **closed**, verified this run, so the guide sends people to check
what is open rather than naming an amount.

**Messaging self-check:** no comparison against cars or driving in the body;
leads with what the bike gives; no sentence defines the bike by what it is not.
**Effort:** small (review) · **Impact:** medium.
**Delivery:** draft committed to `main`; renders nothing until approved.
