DECISIONS FOR KEITH

1. Drive-time guide: a description that earns the click. Recommendation: ship.

That item would have redirected "How far is a 10-minute drive on foot", which had 0 impressions for seven weeks and now gets 732 a week (plus 815 in its translations) at position 6.6.
