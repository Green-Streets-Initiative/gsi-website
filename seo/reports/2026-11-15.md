## Insights

1. **81 of 84 translated guides dropped out of the sitemap** → the pages
   that earn 28% of impressions lost their sitemap entries and their
   language-alternate links. Cause, confirmed: `src/app/sitemap.ts` reads
   `content_item_translations` with no filter, the table reached 1,104 rows
   (534 of them Roam translations; read-only count in
   `seo/data/checks/2026-11-15-sitemap-translations.sql`), and the API returns
   the first 1,000. llms.txt filters by guide id and still lists all 87.
   Locale impressions haven't fallen yet (2,912 → 3,004). → Play 1 (hyp-18).
2. **A quarter of impressions come from outside the US, and US reach was
   flat** (8,005 → 7,982 impressions, 104 → 93 clicks). Brazil alone is 1,092
   impressions and 0 clicks, on the Portuguese bus-transfers guide. → The
   "record impressions" of the last three weeks overstate reach; read US
   impressions from here. The stagnation trigger's basis should move with it
   (December deep-dive, see watch list).
3. **Pages that got a hand "request indexing" were found in a week; pages
   that didn't have 0 impressions after a week.** The three venue pages
   (requested 11-02) took 695 impressions in week one. Logan parking and the
   four events-by-kind pages (sitemap + IndexNow only, 11-08) have none. → A
   33,100-searches-a-month page is waiting on a two-minute step. Play 2
   (hyp-19).
4. **Outcome: the north star fell to 16 (−25%) because the paid budget now
   buys things it doesn't count.** Meta moved from app-download ads to
   calendar-test (324 landings) and lessons-nov (256); `/bike-lessons` has
   taken 352 paid sessions with 0 "activated" and 0 app intent, because a
   lesson request isn't in either list. Organic (2.0%) and Ad Grants (2.6%,
   its best tracked week) both improved on app intent. → The scoreboard
   should count a lesson request and an events email sign-up as outcomes.
   Play 3 (hyp-20).
5. **The event season keeps fading, and events still carry the clicks.**
   Fixed cohort −33% impressions; /events is 12% of impressions and 43% of
   clicks (43 of 100); the 71 new event pages took 14 clicks on 183
   impressions (7.7%). Guides, translations and parking are 68% of
   impressions at 0.47% CTR. Weekly series lose their page each week
   ("boston cafe bikers" at 8, "bike to the sea" at 13, "critical mass
   boston" at 6). → Play 4 (hyp-15, already in the approved calendar plan).
