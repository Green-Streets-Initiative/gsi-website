## Findings

### 1. The ledger has been understating organic traffic by roughly 4×

`scripts/seo/pull-gsc.mjs` derives `totals` by summing Search Console rows from
the **query** dimension. Search Console withholds rare and potentially
identifying queries, so that sum is always a fraction of real traffic. The
**page** dimension for the identical window tells a very different story:

| Window | Query-dim clicks / impr | Page-dim clicks / impr (top 25) | True site totals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-15 → 08-21 | 6 / 249 | 23 / 1,195 | **23 / 1,555** |
| 2026-08-08 → 08-14 | 2 / 252 | — | **9 / 1,204** |
| 2026-08-11 → 08-17 | 3 / 238 | 14 / 712 | not yet pulled |
| 2026-06-09 → 06-15 | 7 / 183 | 27 / 1,054 | not yet pulled |

The gap is consistent across all 17 weekly files. The two true-total rows were
confirmed this run by adding a no-dimension Search Console call on the item 1
branch: for the week of 2026-08-15 the query dimension reports 6 clicks and 249
impressions against a real 23 clicks and 1,555 impressions — **4× the clicks and
6× the impressions**.

The trend line was still directionally usable, but the level was wrong, and
"1 click last week" reads very differently from "5 clicks last week." A second,
subtler bug rides along: `top_pages` was sorted by clicks and truncated to 25,
so a high-impression zero-click page could be dropped entirely — which is very
nearly what happened to `/commute-advisor`, the biggest page on the site.
Both are fixed on the item 1 branch.

### 2. The portfolio's `gsc_patterns` miss most of what we actually rank for

Real queries from this week that should have bucketed and did not:

- `getting around cambridge` (11 impr, pos 18.6) — the `new-to-boston` pattern is
  `get around.*(boston|cambridge|somerville)`, which does not match the gerund
  "getting around". A one-character fix (`get(ting)? around`).
- `malden bike trail` (2 impr), and `/shift/towns/malden-ma` (70 impr) and
  `/shift/towns/saugus-ma` (7 impr) both rank — but **Malden and Saugus are absent
  from the `town-commuters` town list**, which stops at newton.
- `somerville community path`, `watertown cambridge greenway`,
  `bike path davis square`, `somerville bike path` — path / trail / greenway
  queries have **no pattern in any cluster**, yet `/shift/roams/community-path`
  is a live ranking page.
- `commute times`, `commute time`, `10 minute drive to walk`,
  `15 min drive to walk`, `what is a 5 minute drive in walking` — see finding 4.

The repaired patterns are written and verified on the item 2 branch: replayed
against this week's rows they take bucketed coverage from **6% to 88%**,
and what stays unmatched afterwards is genuine noise (`car donation cambridge ma`,
`cambridge hills road`, a couple of quoted-operator searches).

### 3. Nearly two-thirds of what we rank for is people already looking for us

Adding a `brand-navigational` cluster (item 2) makes a split visible that the
ledger could not previously show. Of 195 bucketed query-dimension impressions
this week:

| Bucket | Impressions | Share |
|---|---|---|
| brand-navigational (`green streets`, `greenstreets`, `shift town`, `green line velo`, `recreation sundays`, `frisoli youth center`) | 123 | 63% |

Awareness work is landing — people search our name, our programs, and our events
by name, and we win those. Discovery demand, the thing this routine exists to
grow, is about 48 impressions a week in the query dimension. That is the number
to move, and it is the honest denominator for every future percentage in this
report.

### 4. Our biggest page by impressions is answering an unclaimed question

`/commute-advisor` took **316 impressions at average position 9.2 and converted
one click** this week — the single largest impression source on the site.
`/guides/when-walking-is-faster` took 244 impressions, also one click.

The query family driving them is a drive-time-to-walk-time conversion:
"commute times", "10 minute drive to walk", "15 min drive to walk",
"what is a 5 minute drive in walking". Nobody owns this in our portfolio, and we
have no page that answers it as a plain, extractable statement — the commute
advisor is an interactive tool that requires input before it says anything.

Page titles and canonicals on both pages are correct and well-matched, so this is
a content-shape gap, not a metadata bug:

- `/commute-advisor` → "How long is that drive on foot or by bike? — Commute Advisor"
- `/guides/when-walking-is-faster` → "When walking is actually the faster option — Green Streets Initiative"

presence in live web search for question-shaped queries, referral traffic from
answer-engine domains, and structured-data / llms.txt health. We cannot see
rankings inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude, and we cannot see Google AI
Overview inclusion. This routine will never publish an invented "AI visibility"
score.
