## Findings

### 1. The ledger has been understating organic traffic by roughly 4×

`scripts/seo/pull-gsc.mjs` derives `totals` by summing Search Console rows from
the **query** dimension. Search Console withholds rare and potentially
identifying queries, so that sum is always a fraction of real traffic. The
**page** dimension for the identical window tells a very different story:

| Window | Query-dim clicks / impr | Page-dim clicks / impr |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-15 → 08-21 | 6 / 249 | 23 / 1,195 |
| 2026-08-11 → 08-17 | 3 / 238 | 14 / 712 |
| 2026-08-04 → 08-10 | 2 / 252 | 5 / 932 |
| 2026-06-09 → 06-15 | 7 / 183 | 27 / 1,054 |

The gap is consistent across all 17 weekly files: page-dimension clicks run
3–5× the query-dimension number, impressions 4–5×. The page-dimension figures
above are themselves a **floor** — the file keeps only the top 25 pages.

The trend line was still directionally usable, but the level was wrong, and
"1 click last week" reads very differently from "5 clicks last week." Fixing the
puller is proposed action 1.

### 2. The portfolio's `gsc_patterns` miss most of what we actually rank for

Real queries from this week that should have bucketed and did not:

- `getting around cambridge` (11 impr, pos 18.6) — the `new-to-boston` pattern is
  `get around.*(boston|cambridge|somerville)`, which does not match the gerund
  "getting around". A one-character fix (`get(ting)? around`).
- `malden bike trail` (2 impr), and `/shift/towns/malden-ma` (70 impr) and
  `/shift/towns/saugus-ma` (7 impr) both rank — but **Malden and Saugus are absent
  from the `town-commuters` town list**, which stops at newton.
- `somerville community path`, `watertown cambridge greenway`,
  `bike path davis square`, `cambridge hills road` — path / trail / greenway
  queries have **no pattern in any cluster**, yet `/shift/roams/community-path`
  is a live ranking page.
- `commute times`, `commute time`, `10 minute drive to walk`,
  `15 min drive to walk`, `what is a 5 minute drive in walking` — see finding 3.

Genuinely-out-of-scope noise this week: `car donation cambridge ma` (pos 41.5),
`frisoli youth center`, `green line velo`, and the brand set (`green street`,
`greenstreets`, `green streets initiative` — 59 impressions of navigational
demand, healthy and expected).

### 3. Our biggest page by impressions is answering an unclaimed question

`/commute-advisor` took **316 impressions at average position 9.2 and converted
one click** this week — the single largest impression source on the site.
`/guides/when-walking-is-faster` took 244 impressions, also one click.

The query family driving them is a drive-time-to-walk-time conversion:
"commute times", "10 minute drive to walk", "15 min drive to walk",
"what is a 5 minute drive in walking". Nobody owns this in our portfolio, and we
have no page that answers it as a plain, extractable statement — the commute
advisor is an interactive tool that requires input before it says anything.

Page titles and canonicals on both pages are correct and well-matched, so this is
a content-shape gap, not a metadata bug:

- `/commute-advisor` → "How long is that drive on foot or by bike? — Commute Advisor"
- `/guides/when-walking-is-faster` → "When walking is actually the faster option — Green Streets Initiative"

## Proposed actions

### Item 1 — Record true Search Console totals

**What:** add a no-dimension totals call to `scripts/seo/pull-gsc.mjs`, store it
as `page_totals` alongside the existing query-dimension `totals`, raise the page
`rowLimit` so page-dimension sums stop truncating, and re-run `--mode backfill`
to restate the 16-week history.
**Why:** the ledger is the routine's whole point and it is currently reporting
about a quarter of real traffic (finding 1).
**Effort:** small · **Impact:** high (accuracy of every future report).
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-08-gsc-true-totals`, pushed, not merged.

### Item 2 — Repair the portfolio's query-bucketing patterns

**What:** in `seo/keyword-portfolio.json` —
- `new-to-boston`: `get around` → `get(ting)? around`
- `town-commuters`: add `malden|saugus|melrose|chelsea|revere` to both town alternations
- `town-commuters`: add a paths pattern, `(bike |walking )?(path|trail|greenway|community path)`
- new cluster `drive-time-conversion` (see item 3) with patterns
  `\\d+ ?(min|minute).*(drive|driving).*(walk|bike)`, `(drive|driving).*(in|to) walking`, `commute times?`

**Why:** 94% of query-dimension impressions currently bucket nowhere, so the
per-cluster ledger cannot tell whether any strategy is working (finding 2).
**Effort:** small · **Impact:** high (makes the cluster ledger meaningful).
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-08-portfolio-patterns`, pushed, not merged.

### Item 3 — Claim the drive-time-to-walk-time question

**What:** a micro-guide draft at `seo/drafts/guides/how-far-is-a-ten-minute-drive-on-foot.md`
that answers the question as plain text before offering the tool — a short
conversion table (5 / 10 / 15 / 20-minute drives, in city traffic, expressed in
walking and biking minutes and in miles), each row a one-sentence extractable
answer, with FAQPage schema and a link through to `/commute-advisor`.
**Why:** `/commute-advisor` earns more impressions than any other page at average
position 9.2 and converts almost none of them, because the question gets asked in
the search box and our answer lives behind an input field (finding 3). This is
also the most citable shape we could offer an answer engine.
**Messaging check:** framed as what walking and biking give you — the trip is
shorter than people assume, no parking hunt at the end. No comparison that
diminishes driving.
**Effort:** medium · **Impact:** medium-high.
**Delivery:** draft file committed to `main` (renders nothing live until approved).
