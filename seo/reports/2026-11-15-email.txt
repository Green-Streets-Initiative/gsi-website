PLAYS, RANKED
1. Put the translated guides back on the list we give Google. Recommendation: ship today.
   Why: the Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese guides bring 28% of our impressions (3,004 this week). The sitemap, the list of pages Google reads, showed 84 of them last Sunday and shows 3 now. The cause is confirmed: the code that builds the list reads a table of translations that grew past 1,000 rows when the Roam translations were added, and it only gets the first 1,000. The pages themselves still work. The fix is one small change to how that list is built.
   Cost of waiting: Google hasn't dropped the pages yet. Each week off the list makes that more likely, and it muddies the test of the new Boston titles.
2. Ask Google to index the Logan parking page and the four new event pages (group rides, walking groups, bike repair, this weekend). Recommendation: ship.
   Why: a week after launch all five have zero impressions. The three venue parking pages, where we asked Google by hand, got 695 impressions in their first week. "Logan airport parking" is about 33,100 Massachusetts searches a month. It takes a couple of minutes in Search Console and needs the browser.
   Cost of waiting: both tests end Dec 20 and are spending their weeks unseen.
3. Count a lesson request and an events email sign-up on the scoreboard. Recommendation: ship.
   Why: the two biggest paid campaigns right now (calendar and lessons, 580 visits this week) read as zero because the scoreboard only counts steps toward the app, trip planning and event clicks. The bike lessons page has had 352 paid visits and shows no outcomes at all. The north star itself stays as it is.
   Cost of waiting: we can't tell whether those ads work from this report.
4. One lasting page for each repeating ride (Boston Cafe Bikers, Bike to the Sea, Critical Mass). Recommendation: ship, as the calendar plan you approved lays out.
   Why: event pages are 12% of impressions and 43% of clicks, and a weekly ride's page disappears each week, so its ranking starts over. New event pages got clicked 7.7% of the time this week.
   Cost of waiting: winter is when the weekly rides are most of the calendar.
To ship all four: open Claude Code and say "ship SEO items 1-4" (or "ship SEO item N" / "decline SEO item N").
