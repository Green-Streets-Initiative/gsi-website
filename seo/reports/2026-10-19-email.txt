DECISIONS FOR KEITH

1. Put the drive-time answer on the guide Google already ranks. Recommendation: ship.
Searches like "5 minute drive to walk" now land on "When walking is actually the faster option": 334 impressions a week, 0 clicks. That guide never states the conversion. Our guide that does has had 0 impressions for seven weeks. The current results are spam pages with a wrong answer. The change moves the 5/10/15/20-minute conversion into the guide that ranks and gives it the search title "How long is a 5-minute drive on foot? When walking is faster". The zero-impression guide redirects to it.
Cost of waiting: about 330 impressions a week at 0 clicks, and every week of delay pushes the result back.
To ship: open Claude Code and say "ship SEO item 1" (or "decline SEO item 1").

2. Title the Roams hub for the trail searches people make. Recommendation: ship.
The /shift/roams hub is titled "Shift Roams — Guided Walking, Biking & Transit Routes" and gets 0 impressions. Paid search and last week's keyword analysis show about 19.7k trail searches a month. The new title would be "Bike paths, rail trails and walking loops around Boston", with a list of the routes for search engines. The hub only; the Roam pages you rebuilt on 10-15 stay as they are.
Cost of waiting: about four weeks of foliage-season trail searches.
