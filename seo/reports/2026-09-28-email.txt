WHAT HAPPENED
1. Last week's PostHog jump is partly real. It settled at 55 sessions (71 last week, low 30s all summer), and Search Console now agrees on the size again. Organic traffic has stepped up to about 50 a week. The 71 was that step plus a one-off.

2. The record clicks come with two caveats:
 - One page, our listing for Somerville's Fluff Fest (a MassBike event), got 500 impressions and 0 clicks. People searching for it want the organizer's site. Nothing is wrong with our page.
 - Set that page aside and impressions on pages Google already had fell about 11%. Campus pages halved for the second week in a row, so the September move-in spike is now the leading explanation. Town pages fell 18%, which is the one to watch: a second fall would count as a regression on our core pages.

3. New event pages delivered most of the clicks: 30 new pages, 12 clicks, 2.94% CTR. The top one is Allston-Brighton Open Streets (Oct 4), at position 5.4 with 4 clicks. The event-sitemap experiment shipped only four days into this week, so it gets no credit yet. Verdict Nov 2.

4. Roam pages held their clicks (13 to 12) on fewer impressions, and click-through rate edged up. That's the first reading with two full weeks of the new titles. Verdict Oct 26.

5. The drive-time guide has zero impressions after four weeks. Next week's deep-dive brings structural options before the Oct 12 call.
