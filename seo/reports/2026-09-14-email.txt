Impressions set another record and clicks fell from 30 to 21. Both are true and
they belong in the same sentence: the site was shown 55% more often and clicked
30% less often. Position actually improved, so this is not a ranking problem —
it is a mix problem, and the mix is the whole story this week.

THE FINDING

Twelve /shift/roams pages took 852 impressions and 12 clicks. That is 21% of the
site's impressions and 57% of every click it earned. /shift/roams/emerald-necklace
converted at 6.17% — the best any page on this site has recorded; the site average
is 0.53%.

It is a three-week ramp, not a spike: 1 page / 51 impressions / 1 click in the
week to Aug 28, then 3 / 147 / 3, then 12 / 852 / 12.

And every query behind it — "fresh pond loop", "how long is the fresh pond loop",
"marblehead trail", "emerald necklace trail" — was filed as unmatched noise,
because the portfolio has no cluster for named local routes. Cluster coverage
fell 93% -> 79% in one week for that single reason.

This is the same failure the September deep-dive named about the campus pages,
one month later, with a different page class. The difference is that this time
the invisible class is also the one earning the clicks.

Meanwhile /shift-your-semester took 1,129 impressions and 1 click (0.09%). Volume
held into the second week of term, which is mildly against the "September spike"
theory, but it is not converting. Too early to call either way.

ONE THING THAT ISN'T A MERGE

Searching our own name, the top result is not gogreenstreets.org — it is the
MassCEC directory listing, and that is what the AI summary quoted. It describes
GSI as promoting "sustainable, active transportation", with "sustainable" and
"greener" again in the mission text. Our own copy is clean; /llms.txt opens with
"helps people walk, bike, and ride transit more often — and measures the impact,
trip by trip, community by community", which is exactly right. But we do not
control the listings that outrank us on our own name, and those are what a
language model reads when someone asks it who we are. Worth an email to MassCEC,
and a look at LinkedIn while you are there.

Also, small: when SEO item 2 from last week (seo/2026-09-answer-schema) ships,
change one word first. That branch publishes the /commute-advisor answers as
FAQPage markup, and one of them says "the most sustainable way to build
consistent movement". Fine on the page, in the durable sense — less fine once it
is our machine-readable self-description. "the most durable way" reads the same.
It is noted in the ship steps so it will not be forgotten.
