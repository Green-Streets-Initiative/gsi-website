The title change is live and correct:

> `Battle Road Trail — a 6-mile guided walk route in Lexington · Concord | Shift Roams`
> `6 miles · about 120 min — Walk the path of the American Revolution, from Lexington to Concord`

Note for future runs: outside sources give the Battle Road Trail as 4.9–5 miles
and our page says 6. That is **not** a discrepancy to flag — a Roam is judged
against its own stored route, which includes the approach legs, never against an
outside figure for a trail's full length. The 2026-09-14 audit settled this.

New entrants doing well: `battle-road-trail` (95 impr, 3 clicks, pos 9.2) and
`marblehead-rail-trail` (59 → 112 impr, 0 → 2 clicks).

### 2. Campus impressions halved — the seasonal question has its first answer

`/shift-your-semester` took **541 impressions against 1,129** last week, a 52%
fall, while the rest of the site held. Per school: BU 183, Lesley 114, Tufts 72,
Northeastern 45, Emerson 32, Harvard 32.

Clicks: **2**, and one of those is the hub ranking at position 1.0. CTR on the
school pages is still effectively zero.

This is one week and term has just started, so it is a data point rather than a
verdict — but it is the first evidence for the "September spike" hypothesis
rather than against it. `exp-2026-09-1` is called 2026-10-20 and the honest
framing for that call is now two questions, not one: did the titles lift CTR,
*and* was there a durable audience underneath the September volume.

### 3. PostHog organic more than doubled — with a caveat worth holding

**71 organic sessions** in the week of 2026-09-14, against a trailing-4-week mean
of 32.0. Search Console agrees on direction (clicks +62%) but not on magnitude,
and the ratio between the two moved:

| Week | GSC clicks | PostHog organic sessions | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-28 / 08-31 | 11 | 35 | 3.2 |
| 2026-09-04 / 09-07 | 30 | 29 | 1.0 |
| 2026-09-11 / 09-07 | 21 | 29 | 1.4 |
| **2026-09-18 / 09-14** | **34** | **71** | **2.1** |

What argues it is real: the 71 sessions spread across **25 landing pages** with
no single page dominating (home 13, `/events/shift-your-summer` 7,
`/shift-your-semester/berklee` 5, then a long tail of events, Roams and towns) —
not the shape a bot or a scanner makes. What argues for caution: the two
sources' windows differ (PostHog is Monday-start 09-14 → 09-20, GSC is
Saturday-to-Friday 09-12 → 09-18), and a 2.4× week-over-week jump in any metric
deserves a second week before it is called a trend. **Treat next week's PostHog
number as the confirmation.**

### 4. First answer-engine referrals that are not contest traffic

Three sessions in seven days: **ChatGPT 1, Perplexity 1, Gemini 1.**

Three sessions is noise and this report will not dress it up as more. What makes
it worth recording is the shape: one session each from three different engines,
against a history in which every previous answer-engine referral came from a
single engine pointing at a single contest-rules page and stopped when the
contest ended. This is the first time the pattern looks like discovery. It is
also the first weekly pulse after the Roams and campus sections landed in
`llms.txt`. Both facts are true; neither proves the other.

### 5. "Sustainable" shipped into our machine-readable self-description

The 2026-09-14 report flagged one word to change **before** merging the answer
schema: `/commute-advisor`'s health answer contains "the most sustainable way to
build consistent movement." The schema shipped on 2026-09-17 (`049bc93`) and the
word went with it. It is now published inside `FAQPage` markup, which is exactly
the surface answer engines read back as our own description of ourselves.

Used in the durable sense, so it is defensible as body copy — the org guardrail
is about how we describe active transportation, and this sentence is about
habits lasting. But there is no reason to spend the word here when "durable"
says the same thing. One-word fix, item 1.

### 6. Cluster coverage fell 88% → 73%, and the cause was a cluster fitted to a sample

`roams-trails` was written on 2026-09-14 from the twelve Roams that were indexed
at the time, with the route names hard-coded. As the other thirteen indexed,
their queries fell straight back into unmatched: `battle road trail` (25
impressions across three phrasings, on a page earning 3 clicks), `shining sea
bikeway`, `twin cities rail trail`, and the b-line / salem / bakery-run traffic.

A cluster fitted to what is indexed rather than to what is published decays
every time the class grows. Repaired in item 3, along with a new
`events-programs` cluster for finding 2 above.

### 7. The drive-time guide is genuinely absent, not truncated

`/guides/how-far-is-a-ten-minute-drive-on-foot` does not appear in a 128-row
page table whose floor is **1 impression**. Three weeks live, zero impressions.
Earlier reports could say only "not in the top 40"; this week the statement is
unambiguous. Meanwhile `/guides/when-walking-is-faster` took 130 impressions and
0 clicks, and `/commute-advisor` 183 and 0.

That is the real input to the 2026-10-12 call, and it points away from "wait
longer" and toward the structural options already on the October list.

## Proposed actions

### Item 1 — Spend a different word in the published FAQ markup

**What:** `src/app/commute-advisor/page.tsx` — "the most sustainable way to build
consistent movement" → "the most durable way to build consistent movement".

**Why:** that sentence is now inside `FAQPage` markup (finding 5), so it is part
of what answer engines read back as our self-description. "Durable" carries the
intended meaning — the habit that lasts — without spending the guardrail word.
This was flagged as a pre-merge step on 2026-09-14 and the schema shipped
without it.

**Effort:** trivial · **Impact:** guardrail compliance, not traffic.
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-09-faq-wording`, pushed, not merged. 1 file, +1/−1.
<https://github.com/Green-Streets-Initiative/gsi-website/compare/main...seo/2026-09-faq-wording>

### Item 2 — Put the event pages in the sitemap and llms.txt

**What:** two additions, both reading `loadEventsListing()` —

1. `src/app/sitemap.ts` — emit every approved **upcoming** event page. The
   sitemap currently lists `/events` and `/events/shift-your-summer` and nothing
   else.
2. `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts` — a "What is on" section listing upcoming events
   with date and location, capped at 60 so the file stays an index.

Because `loadEventsListing()` returns upcoming events only, a page leaves both
surfaces the day after it happens; no expiry logic is needed. Both blocks fail
open if the database is unreachable, matching the town, Roam and guide blocks
beside them.

**Why:** 33 event pages rank at positions 3.6–7.7 and convert at **2.34%**
against a 1.03% site average — the best-converting class on the site — and
Google found all of them without our help, through internal links and the share
cards added 2026-09-16. This is catching up with what the crawler already did.
It is also the same move that worked twice: campus pages and Roams both got
their llms.txt section in the last fortnight and llms.txt went 79 → 122 links.

**Verified:** `tsc --noEmit` clean.
**Not verified:** the rendered output. An unattended run cannot start a dev
server, so the generated `<loc>` entries and the "What is on" block have not
been eyeballed against live data. Worth a look at the Vercel preview — check
particularly that event titles read well as link text.
**Effort:** small · **Impact:** medium-high.
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-09-events-discoverable`, pushed, not merged.
2 files, +54/−1.
<https://github.com/Green-Streets-Initiative/gsi-website/compare/main...seo/2026-09-events-discoverable>
