## Decisions for Keith

**SEO item 1: put the drive-time answer on the guide Google already ranks.**
Recommendation: **ship.** Three parts, built on approval:
(1) move the 5/10/15/20-minute conversion and door-to-door section from
`how-far-is-a-ten-minute-drive-on-foot` into `when-walking-is-faster`
(library edit + migration); (2) set that guide's search title to "How long is
a 5-minute drive on foot? When walking is faster" with the description "A
5-minute drive is about a mile: a 20-minute walk or a 6-minute bike ride. The
quick conversion for 5, 10, 15 and 20-minute drives, and the short trips where
walking wins." (same `searchMeta` override as the fares guide); (3) 301 the
zero-impression guide to it. Why: insight 2. 334 impressions a week, 0 clicks,
and a spam field with a wrong answer. Cost of waiting: about 330 impressions a
week at 0 clicks, and Google needs weeks to re-read a title. Reversible.

**SEO item 2: title the Roams hub for the trail searches people make.**
Recommendation: **ship.** One change: search title "Bike paths, rail trails
and walking loops around Boston", a description that names the count of mapped
routes, and an `ItemList` of the routes. The hub only; the Roam pages rebuilt
on 10-15 stay as they are. Why: the hub has 0 impressions under a brand title,
while paid search and the 10-14 keyword analysis show ~19.7k searches a month
on trails. Cost of waiting: the trail season runs through foliage, about four
more weeks. The verdict reads the hub URL only, so it can't confound the Roam
page experiments.
