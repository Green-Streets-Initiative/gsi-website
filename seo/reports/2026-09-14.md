### 2. What those searchers are asking for is a number, and the title does not give it

Four of the top Roam queries are distance questions — `how long is the fresh
pond loop`, `fresh pond loop distance`. The live title is:

> `Fresh Pond Loop — a guided route in Cambridge | Shift Roams`

The distance is in the database (`distance_miles`), is rendered on the page, and
is absent from the title, the meta description, and the structured data. The
page ranks 8.4 for a question it visibly answers and does not say so in the
place the searcher reads.

This is exactly the diagnosis Keith acted on for the campus pages two weeks ago,
and it is the same fix. Item 2.

The JSON-LD on a Roam page today is `NGO`, `AdministrativeArea`, `WebSite`,
`WebPage` — nothing describing the route, its length, its time, or its stops.
Compare `/shift/towns/cambridge-ma`, which carries `City`, `Event`×8, `Place`×8.

### 5. The description answer engines quote about us is not ours

Searching our own brand name, the top-ranked result is not gogreenstreets.org —
it is the **MassCEC company directory listing**, and it is what the AI summary
quoted. That listing describes GSI as working

> "to promote the celebration and use of sustainable, active transportation"

and its mission text uses "sustainable" again plus "greener." Our own copy is
clean — `/llms.txt` opens with "helps people walk, bike, and ride transit more
often — and measures the impact, trip by trip, community by community," which is
exactly on strategy. A repo-wide grep found "sustainable" in only two places on
the site, neither of them a description of the organization.

We control our pages. We do not control the third-party listings that outrank
them on our own name, and those are what a language model reads when someone
asks it what Green Streets Initiative is. This is not a code fix; it is a request
to MassCEC (and worth checking LinkedIn and the other directories at the same
time). Noted below as a non-code action.

### Item 1 — Bucket the Roam pages: add a `roams-trails` cluster

**What:** a new priority-1 cluster in `seo/keyword-portfolio.json` for named
local routes — Fresh Pond, the Emerald Necklace, the Minuteman, the Community

**Why:** 852 impressions and 57% of the site's clicks are currently filed as
noise. Coverage of sampled query impressions goes **79% → 88%** and unmatched
falls 117 → 64. It also corrects `town-commuters`, which is currently being
credited with 72 impressions that belong to Roams.

**Verified:** replayed against this week's real queries with
`scripts/seo/analyze.mjs --section clusters` — the named-route queries move into
the new cluster, the non-brand trigger total is unchanged at 442 (a
reclassification within non-brand, not new volume), and nothing was stolen from
`students-campus` or `brand-navigational`. Remaining unmatched is genuine
miscellany.
**Effort:** small · **Impact:** measurement — high, traffic — none directly.
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-09-roams-cluster`, pushed, not merged. 1 file, +31.
<https://github.com/Green-Streets-Initiative/gsi-website/compare/main...seo/2026-09-roams-cluster>

### Item 2 — Put the distance in the Roam title, and describe the route to answer engines

**What:** in `src/app/shift/roams/[id]/page.tsx` —

- the title leads with the distance: `Fresh Pond Loop — a 2.3-mile guided walk
  route in Cambridge | Shift Roams`;
- the meta description leads with the facts, then the editorial hook:
  `2.3 miles · about 50 min — <hook>`;
- JSON-LD gains `additionalProperty` entries for distance / time / stops, a
  `TouristTrip` `mainEntity` whose `itinerary` lists the required stops with
  coordinates, and a `BreadcrumbList`.

Everything is derived from fields the page already renders, so the markup cannot
drift from the visible copy. When `distance_miles` is null the wording falls back
to exactly what ships today.

**Why:** these pages rank ~8 for questions that are requests for a number, and
the number is the one thing the title withholds. Same hypothesis as
exp-2026-09-1, on the page class that has already proved it converts.

**Verified:** `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
**Not verified:** the rendered page. Dev servers cannot be started from an
unattended run, so the title and description strings have not been eyeballed
against live Supabase data. Worth a look at the Vercel preview before merging —
specifically that the hook does not read awkwardly after the facts prefix.
**Judgment call Keith may want to veto:** prefixing the editorial hook with
`2.3 miles · about 50 min —` puts numbers ahead of voice in the SERP snippet. It
is the right trade for a distance query and the wrong one if the hooks are doing
brand work. Easy to drop that one line and keep the title change.
**Effort:** small · **Impact:** medium-high.
**Delivery:** branch `seo/2026-09-roam-titles`, pushed, not merged. 1 file, +73/−3.
<https://github.com/Green-Streets-Initiative/gsi-website/compare/main...seo/2026-09-roam-titles>
