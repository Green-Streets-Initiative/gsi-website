## Insights

1. **The event season is ending and guides took over the impressions.**
   /events fell 4,159 → 2,071 (fixed cohort −52%, CTR held at 2.4%), while
   /guides grew to 2,656 and the three translation classes to 2,912. Guides
   and translations are now **53% of impressions at ~0.45% CTR**; events were
   40% at 2.5% a week ago. → The site-wide CTR drop (1.40% → 1.10%) is mix,
   not decay. The click lever for winter is guide and locale CTR. (hyp-12)
2. **The translations rank for generic searches from everywhere.**
   `/pt-BR/guides/how-bus-transfers-work` 614 impr / 2 clicks at 7.7 (generic
   "baldeação"), the three drive-time translations 980 impr / 1 click. Titles
   are the translated English title plus "— Green Streets Initiative", with no
   place in them. → One template change in `translatedGuideMetadata` can put
   Boston in all 84 titles. Decision 1.
3. **Organic search barely reaches the app (0.5%); paid social carries the
   north star.** 27 of 29 app-intent sessions in the week of 10-26 came from
   Meta (one_line_test, calendar-test, lessons-nov). Organic landings: home
   5.2%, towns 6.0%, /shift 37.5% (3 of 8), events 0.5%, guides 0 of 35.
   → Outcome: organic needs a guide-reader next step. hyp-10 (guide next
   step) rises now that guides are the biggest organic class; re-scored.
4. **Venue parking pages got found in their first week** (695 impr, pos 9–12)
   with no ads running. → The organic half of exp-2026-11-3 is working before
   the paid half launches. No action; read at verdict.
5. **The non-brand trigger is losing its sample.** 7% of impressions carried
   a query this week. → Watch item: if the sampled share stays under 15% for
   two more weeks, propose moving the trigger to page-class impressions
   (all classes except home/about/brand) in the December deep-dive.
