## Insights

1. **Event listings are the growth engine, and they compound.** /events is 40% of
   impressions at 2.5% CTR, nearly double the site's 1.40%, with ~700 new-page
   impressions every week. → Getting each listing up early (hyp-07) is still the
   highest-volume lever. Moving Together (1,607 impr, 36 clicks at pos 3.7) shows
   a conference or city day can be a third of a week.
2. **Event visitors act on the event, not the app.** 11% click Register, 0.6%
   reach the app; town-page landings reach the app at 6.3% (4 of 63) and home
   at 4.8%. → exp-2026-11-1 (item 3): link events to their town page.
3. **The drive-time guide came alive, and its snippet gives the answer away.**
   0 impressions for seven weeks, now 732/week at pos 6.6 in English plus 815
   across /pt-BR, /es and /zh-Hans, all at ~0% CTR. The meta description states
   the answer in full. → Withdraw last report's item 1, which would have
   redirected this page. Replace it with a description that points at the
   table (item 1, exp-2026-11-2).
4. **The translations are found, mostly by the wrong searchers.** 84 locale guides
   took 2,196 impr / 3 clicks. The top one ranks for generic Portuguese "baldeação
   ônibus" with no Boston in the title. → hyp-12: put Boston/MBTA in locale titles
   and measure clicks, not impressions.
5. **Roam pages convert search better since the rebuild** (cohort CTR 0.88% → 1.56%),
   and the hub still has a brand title nobody searches. → item 2 stands unchanged.

## Decisions (≤ 3)

1. **Drive-time guide: a description that earns the click.** Ship. Replaces

2. **Roams hub search title + ItemList.** Ship. Carried from 10-19 unchanged.
