## Insights (X is happening → which means Y)

1. **The Open Streets listing earned its biggest week on the event weekend
   itself (1,522 impressions, 28 clicks), and the rest of the site still set a
   record without it (75 clicks).** → Event growth is not one page.
   Ex-Allston /events converts at 2.43%, above the class's own 2.06% cohort
   rate. Roslindale (10-18) is the next read on hyp-07: 197 impressions at
   5.5 a week out.
2. **Paid search is telling us about T-fare demand that organic misses.** Ad
   Grants' top terms this week: "boston public transportation", "how to pay
   for the t in boston" (×2 variants), "how to ride the t in boston", "boston
   t pass". `/guides/how-to-pay-for-the-t` answers this exactly and earned 0
   organic impressions. Its title has no Boston, MBTA or CharlieCard. The live
   SERP for the query is scraped/spam pages and an NBC Boston news item: a
   weak field. → A title fix is the cheapest test we have this month (SEO item 1),
   and if it works, organic can take that query off the grant's budget.
3. **Organic app intent hit 4.2% (4 of 96), but not from events.** The four
   came from home, town and campus pages and /shift. Organic event landings
   reached the app 2 of 174 times since 09-14 (1.1%), and 0 of the ~48 new
   event landings this week. → exp-2026-09-8 (event invitation) is leaning
   lost at 10-26. Pages that are *about* moving differently (towns, campus)
   convert. Pages about a single outing don't. That shapes the next
   conversion bet: point event traffic at the town page for that
   neighbourhood rather than straight at the app.
4. **Ad Grants traffic lands on guides and does not convert (0 of 50 /guides
   paid sessions to app intent).** The grant is compliant on CTR (6.86% month
   to date) but has 0 conversions, which is also a grant rule. → Guide readers
   want the answer. A guide-specific next step is in the backlog (hyp-10, low
   ICE) until the 10-26 invite verdicts say whether page-level invites convert
   at all. The conversion fix itself belongs to the `ad-grants-review`
   routine.
5. **Campus held its floor and found a year-round question.** Semester cohort
   +16%, and "brandeis shuttle / schedule / to boston" ≈ 30 impressions at
   position ~8, unmatched by the portfolio. → Shuttle-to-Boston answers are a
   local, non-seasonal need (hyp-09). Add the pattern at the November
   deep-dive.

## Instruments (committed this run, straight to main, no site code)

- `scripts/seo/inspect.mjs`: `queries|pages <regex>` over the newest GSC
  file, `meta <path>` for a live page's title/description/canonical/JSON-LD,
  and `listed <slug…>` for sitemap + llms.txt + /guides presence.
- `scripts/seo/branch.mjs`: `start | check | finish | rebase` for `seo/…`
  branches in a worktree under `.claude/worktrees/`, never the shared tree.
- Both run under the existing `Bash(node scripts/seo/:*)` rule. The new hard
  rules in `seo/methodology.md` (no `cd … &&` prefix, no raw grep/curl/sed)
  are what stop the prompts. This run's prompts came from breaking them, not
  from missing allow rules.
