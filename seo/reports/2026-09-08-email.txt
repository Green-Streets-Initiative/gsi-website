=== WHAT HAPPENED ===

The campus pages are the whole story. Eleven /shift-your-semester pages
took 917 impressions this week -- 36% of everything the site earned --
at an average position around 9, for 1 click.

  /shift-your-semester/mit                 239 impr, pos 9.8
  /shift-your-semester/boston-university   172 impr, pos 7.4
  /shift-your-semester/tufts               132 impr, pos 11.3
  /shift-your-semester/emerson              88 impr, pos 6.5
  ...eleven pages in total

And the queries behind them -- 'bu cycle kitchen' (68 impressions at
position 5.9), 'mit t pass', 'tufts safe ride', 'quad bikes harvard',
'suffolk mbta pass', 'northeastern blue bike discount' -- were every one
of them filed as unmatched noise, because the portfolio had no cluster
for students. Two previous reports read the biggest surface on the site
as clutter. That is the measurement lesson of the month, and it is fixed:
students-campus is now a priority-1 cluster.

You got there first. Your three commits mid-run (e0635fb, 14cba22,
a2c34a2) gave all twelve schools titles written for the campus question
instead of the campaign. That is now exp-2026-09-1 in the ledger --
shipped 2026-09-08, base 917 impressions / 0 clicks, verdict due
2026-10-20. It is the first experiment on the clock with a real
hypothesis, and the most important number of the autumn.
